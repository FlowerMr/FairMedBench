# Limitations

- The benchmark is not a clinical decision-support system.
- Dataset-level performance does not establish clinical utility or safety.
- Fairness metrics are descriptive and depend on the intended task.
- Subgroup labels can be noisy or incomplete.
- Small subgroups produce unstable estimates.
- Explanation maps are not causal explanations.
- Deletion-based faithfulness depends on the perturbation and masking strategy.
- Calibration depends on the evaluation distribution.
- DDI is a curated clinical dataset and should not be treated as representative of all clinical populations.
- The DDI images are not redistributed by this repository because the dataset terms restrict redistribution.
- A stronger research study should add bootstrap confidence intervals, patient-level splits, external validation, and statistical uncertainty around subgroup gaps.
